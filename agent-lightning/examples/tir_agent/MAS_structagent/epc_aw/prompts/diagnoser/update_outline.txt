## STEP 3 — Epistemic Reorientation & Repair (REQUIRED)

You MUST:

1. **Remove** all steps that depend on invalid assumptions.
2. **Remove** all steps already **solved** in the Previous ExecutionOutline.
3. **Add at least ONE new step** that:

   - Operates at a **different evidence level** than removed steps.
   - Targets a **new epistemic objective**, chosen from:
     - scope delimitation  
     - non-existence confirmation  
     - evidence availability assessment  
     - provenance validation
   - Does **not** directly answer the original Question.
   - Produces a **concrete intermediate artifact**
     (e.g., citation list, paper ID, section index, comparison table).

- Reference list extraction  
- PDF full-text access
